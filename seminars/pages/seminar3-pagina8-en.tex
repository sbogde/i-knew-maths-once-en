Let $A,B$ be sets and $A'\subseteq A$. For
$f,g\in\operatorname{Hom}(A,B)$, define
\[
 f\sim g\overset{\mathrm{def}}\iff f|_{A'}=g|_{A'}.
\]
Show that $\sim$ is an equivalence relation and that
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(A,B)/{\sim}
 \ \overset{\mathrm{bij}}{\cong}\ \operatorname{Hom}(A',B).
\]
An element of the quotient is called in the notes a \emph{germ}
of $f$ on $A'$.

\medskip
\noindent\underline{Proof.}
Consider the canonical projection $p$ and the restriction map $h$:
\[
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=huge,row sep=large]
 \operatorname{Hom}(A,B)
    \arrow[r,"p"]\arrow[d,"h"']
  & \operatorname{Hom}(A,B)/{\sim}
       \arrow[dl,dashed,"\overline h"] \\
 \operatorname{Hom}(A',B) &
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad h(f)=f|_{A'}.
\]
The relation is the equivalence relation induced by $h$:
\[
 \sim\;=R_h,
 \qquad f\sim g\iff h(f)=h(g)
 \iff f|_{A'}=g|_{A'}.
\]
Therefore the induced map $\overline h$ is injective.

\medskip
\noindent For every map $t:A'\longrightarrow B$, we seek a map
$f:A\longrightarrow B$ such that $f|_{A'}=t$.
Choose $a\in B$ and put
\[
 f(x)=
 \begin{cases}
   t(x),&x\in A',\\
   a,&x\in A\setminus A'.
 \end{cases}
\]
This is the extension indicated by the diagram:
\[
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=large,row sep=large]
 A'\arrow[r,hook,"i"]\arrow[d,"t"']
    & A\arrow[dl,dashed,"f"]\\
 B &
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad f\circ i=t.
\]
Thus, as written at the end of the page,
\[
 \forall\,g:A'\longrightarrow B,\quad
 \exists\,f:A\longrightarrow B
 \quad\text{such that}\quad f|_{A'}=g.
\]
